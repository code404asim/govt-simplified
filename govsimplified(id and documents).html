FAQ — Common Questions

For renewal or modification you can use NADRA Pak-Identity portal (select renewal/issue option), or visit a NADRA Registration Centre with documents and biometrics.

You need your old passport, CNIC, passport photos or updated biometric info. Renewals can often be started on DGIP MRP portal.

Visit the official BISP or Ehsaas portal and enter your CNIC or use the helpline codes (where available) to check eligibility and status.

Domicile / PRC certificates are issued by District/Deputy Commissioner offices or via provincial online portals depending on the province.
